Days Between Dates Calculator

Count the days between any two dates, with weeks, months and business days (Monday to Friday).

How to count the days between two dates

Count the days from the start date up to the end date. Most people do not count the start day itself. For example, from January 1 to January 10 is 9 days. If you want to count both the first and last day (for example, the length of a trip or an event), tick "Include the end date" and the answer becomes 10.

Business days

Business days are Monday to Friday. This calculator does not remove public holidays, so subtract any holidays that fall in your date range yourself.

Frequently asked questions

How many days are there between two dates?

Subtract the earlier date from the later one. By default the start day is not counted, so January 1 to January 10 is 9 days. Tick "Include the end date" to count both days.

How many business days are in a year?

There are usually 260 or 261 weekdays (Monday to Friday) in a year. Subtract public holidays to get working days.

How many days until Christmas?

Set the start date to today and the end date to December 25. The calculator opens with this already filled in.

How many weeks are in a year?

A year has 52 weeks and 1 day, or 52 weeks and 2 days in a leap year.
