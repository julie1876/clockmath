Oregon Break Laws: Meal and Rest Break Rules

Oregon break law in short: Meal break: 30 min for 6+ hr shifts. Rest breaks: Paid 10 min per 4 hrs worked.

Meal and lunch breaks in Oregon

Employees working 6 hours or more get an unpaid, duty-free 30-minute meal period. When the work makes it impossible to fully relieve the employee, the meal period must be paid.

Rest breaks in Oregon

Employees get a paid 10-minute rest break for every 4-hour work period (or major part of one), as close to the middle as practical. Rest breaks can't be added to the meal period or taken at the start or end of the shift.

Good to know

  • Union-covered employees are excluded.
  • Adults working alone in a retail or service business for less than 5 hours can be exempt from rest breaks.

What federal law says (applies in every state)

  • Federal law doesn't require employers to give meal or rest breaks.
  • If your employer does give short breaks (usually 5 to 20 minutes), they must be paid.
  • Meal breaks of 30 minutes or more can be unpaid, but only if you're fully relieved of work. If you have to keep working while you eat, that time must be paid.
  • Most employers must give reasonable break time and a private space (not a bathroom) for nursing employees to pump breast milk for up to a year after a baby's birth.
