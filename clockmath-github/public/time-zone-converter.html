US time zone differences

The mainland US has four main time zones. Each one is 1 hour behind the zone to its east:

  • Eastern (New York): 12:00 PM
  • Central (Chicago): 11:00 AM
  • Mountain (Denver): 10:00 AM
  • Pacific (Los Angeles): 9:00 AM

Most of the US moves clocks forward 1 hour in March and back 1 hour in November for daylight saving time. Arizona (except the Navajo Nation) and Hawaii do not, so their difference from other states changes during the year. This converter accounts for daylight saving time automatically for the date you pick.

Frequently asked questions

If it is 12 PM Eastern, what time is it Pacific?

When it is 12:00 PM Eastern Time, it is 9:00 AM Pacific Time. Pacific is 3 hours behind Eastern.

How many hours ahead is Eastern from Central?

Eastern Time is 1 hour ahead of Central Time. 3:00 PM Central is 4:00 PM Eastern.

Does Arizona change its clocks?

Most of Arizona does not use daylight saving time. In summer it matches Pacific Time; in winter it matches Mountain Time.

Does this converter handle daylight saving time?

Yes. It uses your browser's built-in time zone data, so the result is correct for the date you choose.
