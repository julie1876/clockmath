How to calculate hours worked with a lunch break

Work out the time before lunch and the time after lunch, then add them together.

  • Morning: 8:00 AM to 12:00 PM is 4 hours.
  • Afternoon: 12:30 PM to 4:30 PM is 4 hours.
  • Total: 8 hours worked, with a 30-minute lunch.

Another way is to work out the full day from clock-in to clock-out, then subtract the length of lunch. Both ways give the same answer.

Frequently asked questions

How many hours is 8 to 4:30 with a 30-minute lunch?

8:00 AM to 4:30 PM is 8 hours 30 minutes. Minus a 30-minute lunch, that is 8 hours worked.

If I take lunch at 12:00 for 30 minutes, when do I come back?

A 30-minute lunch that starts at 12:00 PM ends at 12:30 PM. Use the "When does my lunch end?" tab for any start time and length.

Is a 30-minute lunch break paid?

Usually not, as long as you are fully relieved of work during it. Breaks of 20 minutes or less are generally paid. Rules vary by state and employer.

How do I subtract lunch from my hours?

Find the total time from clock-in to clock-out, then subtract the length of your lunch. For example, 9 AM to 5 PM is 8 hours; minus a 1-hour lunch is 7 hours.
