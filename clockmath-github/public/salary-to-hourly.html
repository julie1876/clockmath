Turn an hourly wage into a yearly salary, or a salary into an hourly rate, with weekly, biweekly and monthly pay.

How to convert hourly pay to a yearly salary

Multiply your hourly wage by the hours you work in a year. Full time is usually 40 hours a week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. So $20 an hour × 2,080 = $41,600 a year.

A quick shortcut: double your hourly wage and add three zeros. $20 an hour is roughly $40,000 a year.

How to convert a salary to an hourly wage

Divide the yearly salary by 2,080. A $50,000 salary is $50,000 ÷ 2,080 = $24.04 an hour.

Frequently asked questions

How much is $20 an hour a year?

$20 an hour is $41,600 a year before taxes, based on 40 hours a week for 52 weeks. That is about $3,467 a month.

How much is $50,000 a year per hour?

$50,000 a year is about $24.04 an hour, based on 2,080 working hours a year.

How many working hours are in a year?

A full-time year is usually 2,080 hours: 40 hours a week × 52 weeks. With two weeks of unpaid time off, it is 2,000 hours.

Is $25 an hour good pay?

$25 an hour is $52,000 a year for full-time work. Whether that is good depends a lot on where you live and your living costs.
