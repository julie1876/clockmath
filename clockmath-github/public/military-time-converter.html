How to convert to military time

  • AM times: keep the hour and add a leading zero if needed. 7:15 AM is 0715.
  • 12 AM (midnight): is 0000. 12:45 AM is 0045.
  • 12 PM (noon): stays 1200.
  • PM times after noon: add 12 to the hour. 3:30 PM is 1530.

To go back, subtract 12 from any hour above 12. 2100 is 9:00 PM. Military time is usually said as "fourteen thirty" for 1430, and "zero seven hundred" for 0700.

Frequently asked questions

What is 1700 in military time?

1700 is 5:00 PM. Subtract 12 from any hour above 12 to get the PM time.

What is midnight in military time?

Midnight is 0000. One minute after midnight is 0001, and 12:30 AM is 0030.

How do you say military time out loud?

Say the four digits as two pairs. 1430 is "fourteen thirty", 0900 is "zero nine hundred", and 2000 is "twenty hundred".

Is military time the same as 24-hour time?

Nearly. Both run from 00:00 to 23:59. Military time is usually written without a colon (1430), while 24-hour time often has one (14:30).
