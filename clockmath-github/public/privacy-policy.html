Privacy Policy

Short version: the calculators run in your browser and we don't collect what you type.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClockMath ("we", "us") at clockmathtools.com handles information when you use this website.

Information you enter in the calculators

The calculators run entirely in your web browser. Times, rates and other values you type are not sent to or stored on our servers. The Timesheet Calculator saves your entries in your browser's local storage so they are still there when you return on the same device. You can remove them at any time with the "Clear all" button or by clearing your browser data.

Advertising and cookies

We use third-party advertising companies, including Google, to show ads on this site. These companies may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to show ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at www.aboutads.info.
  • To learn more, see how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Where required by law (for example, for visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and some US states), you will be asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choices at any time.

Analytics and server logs

Our hosting provider may collect standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and pages visited, to keep the site secure and working. We use Google Analytics to understand how many people visit and which pages are popular. It uses cookies and collects information such as pages visited, device type and general location, but it doesn't tell us who you are. We do not sell your personal information.

Children

This site is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Links to other sites

This site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

If you have questions about this policy, email contact@clockmathtools.com.
