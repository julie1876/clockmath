How to calculate overtime pay

  1. Find your overtime rate: hourly rate × 1.5. At $20 an hour, overtime is $30 an hour.
  2. Find your overtime hours: total hours minus 40. 45 hours is 5 overtime hours.
  3. Regular pay: 40 × $20 = $800.
  4. Overtime pay: 5 × $30 = $150.
  5. Total: $800 + $150 = $950 before taxes.

Frequently asked questions

What is time and a half for $20 an hour?

Time and a half for $20 an hour is $30 an hour. Multiply your hourly rate by 1.5.

How is overtime calculated?

Hours over 40 in a workweek are paid at 1.5 times your regular rate. Multiply your overtime hours by your rate times 1.5, then add your regular pay for the first 40 hours.

How much is 10 hours of overtime at $15 an hour?

Your overtime rate is $22.50, so 10 overtime hours is $225, on top of your regular pay.

Is overtime taxed more?

No. Overtime is taxed at the same rates as regular pay. A bigger paycheck can have more withheld, but that is settled when you file your tax return.
