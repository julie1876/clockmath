How to convert minutes to decimal hours

Divide the minutes by 60, then add the whole hours. For example, 7 hours 45 minutes is 7 + (45 ÷ 60) = 7.75 hours.

To go the other way, multiply the decimal part by 60. 7.75 hours is 7 hours and 0.75 × 60 = 45 minutes.

Payroll systems use decimal hours because they are easy to multiply by a pay rate: 7.75 hours × $20 = $155.

Frequently asked questions

What is 15 minutes in decimal hours?

15 minutes is 0.25 hours. 30 minutes is 0.5, and 45 minutes is 0.75.

What is 7.5 hours in hours and minutes?

7.5 hours is 7 hours 30 minutes. Multiply the decimal part (0.5) by 60 to get the minutes.

How do I convert minutes to decimal?

Divide the minutes by 60. For example, 20 minutes ÷ 60 = 0.33 hours.

What is 8.25 hours in time?

8.25 hours is 8 hours 15 minutes, because 0.25 × 60 = 15.
